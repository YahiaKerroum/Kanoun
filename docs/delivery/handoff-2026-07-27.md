  story.

- Customer and administration web applications are foundations only.
- The physical schema currently contains platform tables only.
- Product authorization, tenant isolation, last-administrator protection,
  concurrency, retry, and session-revocation evidence begins in Slice 002.
- Production deployment remains blocked until proposed `ADR-0007` is accepted
  or superseded.
