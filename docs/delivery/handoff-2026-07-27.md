## Slice 003 delivered

- Loginless employee profiles, branch employment, profile lifecycle, and later
  account attachment without changing the employee identifier.
- Versioned grants-only permission sets, per-branch scopes, delegation-subset
  enforcement, immutable copy-on-apply templates, last-administrator
  protection, and affected-session revocation.
- Append-only restaurant/branch feature configuration versions, fixed MVP
  strategies, dependency validation, optimistic concurrency, transactional
  audit/outbox evidence, and capability resolution.
- Capability-aware MISE staff navigation. It loads the authenticated branch
  capability response and shows a destination only when its permission and
  feature requirements are both effective. Tablet navigation retains every
  authorized destination.
- Administration employee, permission-template, and feature-configuration
  flows with actionable duplicate-email conflicts and runtime-validated HTTP
  adapters.
- Private support inspection through a hashed, time-limited grant token. The
  trusted adapter requires recent platform authentication, explicit
  `two_person` approval, a distinct approver, and an approval reference. It is
  limited to the implemented restaurant/branch snapshot and has no
  emergency-policy bypass.

## Explicit boundaries

- Slice 003 does not claim `AC-US-B01-03` downstream automatic actions or
  notifications. It stores configuration, validates dependencies, emits
  outbox evidence, and filters navigation; later module slices enforce the
  setting when they create work.
- Slice 003 does not claim `AC-US-C07-02` task proxying or assignment/report
  consumers that do not exist. It establishes the stable loginless employee
  reference and account-attachment path.
- Operational order, table, menu, kitchen, payment, notification, reporting,
  audit-query, and task screens remain deferred to their owning slices. The
  staff shell does not fabricate their data or actions.
- The support adapter creates no derived support session or real-time
  subscription. Expiry and revocation terminate the implemented access by
  invalidating the direct grant token. The revocation event does not claim
  nonexistent sessions or subscriptions were terminated.
- The private support secret authenticates a trusted platform control-plane
  caller; upstream platform IAM remains responsible for proving operator and
  approver identities associated with the supplied approval reference.
