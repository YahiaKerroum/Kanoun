Bricolage Grotesque headings, Plus Jakarta Sans body text, restrained panels,

`MISE` is still a working product name, not an approved product decision.

  broader employee/permission UI owned by Slice 003.

  delivery adapter; production configuration fails closed without it.
- Production deployment remains blocked until proposed `ADR-0007` is accepted
  or superseded.
