# Staff Quick-Start Guide (proposed)

For any general staff, kitchen, or cashier employee joining the pilot.

## Accept your invitation

1. Open the acceptance link your manager sent you (one-time use).
2. Set your password.
3. You are signed in automatically after acceptance.

## Sign in and sign out

- Sign in at the Staff URL with your business code, email, and password.
- Your name, restaurant/branch, and effective responsibilities show in the
  header at all times.
- Sign out from the same header menu — this ends your session immediately,
  on this device only.

## What you'll see

Staff shows only what your permissions and your branch's enabled features
allow. A destination you cannot use will not appear in navigation, but if
you follow an old link to it you get a clear, safe message rather than a
broken page.

- **Orders**: current active orders, filterable by state, table, and time.
- **Kitchen**: your branch's queued and preparing items, grouped, with
  elapsed time and item notes at a size readable from a normal kitchen
  working distance.
- **Payments**: bill requests and payment/refund recording for orders
  ready to close.

## If something looks wrong

- A stale or disconnected screen tells you it's stale rather than silently
  showing old data; refresh or wait for automatic reconnect.
- A failed action shows a clear pending/failed state — it is safe to retry
  with the same action; the system prevents duplicate effects.
- Report anything that seems broken using
  `docs/operations/pilot/support-escalation.md`.
